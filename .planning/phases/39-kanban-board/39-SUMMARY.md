## OQ-77, OQ-78, OQ-79 as built

- OQ-77 (final column names): the ten default names are in force in `KanbanColumn`; the owner has not confirmed them.
- OQ-78 (mapping): the default mapping is built (lifecycle to runtime status to column). It sits in `AgentRowStatusMap` and `RuntimeStatusMap`, not in the view.
- OQ-79 (closed or ended session): a card is never removed by the board. Closing a session (`SessionEngineSet.CloseSessionAsync`) leaves a Completed session Completed and records every other session Paused (Created, Running, Paused, Failed or Lost), and marks it closed. A closed Paused session shows in Archived, so a closed Failed or Lost session ends in Archived too. A session that ended by itself and has not been closed stays in its own column: Completed, Failed (an omp terminal that exited with a non-zero code) or Unreachable. A Completed session stays in Completed after a close. After a restart the head marks every Paused record closed, so those cards show in Archived. The card of a Completed session leaves the board only when the Runtime page's retention window removes the session record (`SessionLifecycleSweeper`, default 7 days), because the board follows the catalogue.
