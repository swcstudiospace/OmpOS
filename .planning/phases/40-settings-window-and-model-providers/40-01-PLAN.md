# Phase 40 Plan 01: Settings Window and Model Providers

Inputs: `40-CONTEXT.md` (decisions D40-1 to D40-12), ROADMAP.md Phase 40 (criteria 1 to 7) and DR-5c, DR-6, REQUIREMENTS.md (VX-05, AL-19, AL-20), `docs/plans/vision-research/cursor-settings-window.md` (the 34 rows), the provider research files under `docs/plans/vision-research/`, the probe evidence in `docs/runbooks/evidence/phase-40-omp-providers-2026-10-26/`.

This record lists the units that were built, from the commits. It was written after the work and is not a plan that was followed in advance. The probe of how omp holds providers, credentials and the default model came first and moved the design from "Autonogrammer keeps a provider registry and keys" to "Autonogrammer edits what omp reads" (OQ-92).
