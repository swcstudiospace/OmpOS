# Phase 49 Plan 01: Live Steering

Inputs: `49-CONTEXT.md` (decisions D49-1 to D49-8), ROADMAP.md Phase 49 (criteria 1 to 5) and DR-4, REQUIREMENTS.md (AL-15, AL-16), `docs/open-questions.md` OQ-81 and OQ-66, the probe evidence in `docs/runbooks/evidence/phase-48-omp-plugins-2026-10-26/acp-auth-and-steering.md`.

This record lists the units that were built, from the commits. It was written after the work and is not a plan that was followed in advance. The inbox mechanism of Phase 48 came first and was the default of the ROADMAP; the discovery that omp 18.12.6 implements `_session/steering` came during the build and changed the order of the mechanisms.
