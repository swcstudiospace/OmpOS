## 1. Common registration for every project (the 13-step recipe of report 09 section 2)

Per source project: csproj with only `ItemGroup`s (no `PropertyGroup`; `Directory.Build.props` gives the rest), `InternalsVisibleTo Include="<name>.Tests"`, `AssemblyMarker.cs`; one `<Project>` line in `Autonogrammer.slnx` under `/src/`; one path in `Autonogrammer.Linux.slnf`; one line in `LayerMap.Layers`; one `ProjectReference` in `tests/Autonogrammer.ArchitectureTests/Autonogrammer.ArchitectureTests.csproj`; committed `packages.lock.json` (and the relocked locks of every dependent project); a row in `docs/architecture.md`; a `CHANGELOG.md` entry under Unreleased. Per test project: csproj whose name ends in `Tests` referencing the project under test, `xunit.v3`, `Shouldly`, `coverlet.MTP`, `Microsoft.Testing.Extensions.TrxReport`; one line each in the slnx (`/tests/`) and the slnf; its own `packages.lock.json`; it is not in `LayerMap` (tests are not product assemblies). After all projects exist: `dotnet restore Autonogrammer.Linux.slnf --force-evaluate -p:RestoreLockedMode=false` once, then `python3 scripts/legal/generate-third-party-notices.py` once (packages change), then the locked restore check.
